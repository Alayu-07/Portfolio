    How I work

    Operating principles from 18 years of teams

    Define the standard first

    From quality systems: agree on what “done” looks like before building — clear scope, clear acceptance criteria.

    Measure, then improve

    Check the result against the standard, find the biggest gap, fix that, repeat. No guesswork.

    Safety and clarity over shortcuts

    Years of safety leadership: no hidden risks, readable code, and changes that are easy to review and roll back.

    Lead the people, not just the process

    18 years of teams taught me that communication, documentation, and handover decide whether work survives me.
